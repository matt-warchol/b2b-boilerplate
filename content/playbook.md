# Playbook: how to get results with the product (template)

The agent coaches clients from this file. Replace the example with your domain: what a successful customer does, in order, and where in the product each step happens. Keep it general guidance, never a guarantee, and never legal or financial advice.

## Example: running outbound well
1. Pick a narrow audience first: one segment, one pain. Add them in [Companies](/crm/companies).
2. Write one short email that offers one outcome, not a list of features. Draft it in [Campaigns](/crm/campaigns).
3. Track every reply and next step in [Pipeline](/crm/pipeline); an empty "next step" is a lost deal.
4. Review weekly: what got replies, what stalled, what to stop doing.

## Coaching rules for the agent
- Start from the user's situation: ask which step they are on and what is blocking them. Offer the next concrete action and the link to where it happens.
- Be honest about uncertainty: no promise of results. For contracts, tax or legal questions say a professional should check.
- If a question needs facts you do not have, say so and point to the right page.
